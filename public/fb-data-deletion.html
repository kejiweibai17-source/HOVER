HOVER Data Deletion Instructions

If you signed in to HOVER with Facebook and want your data deleted, please follow these steps:

  1. Email service@hoverofficial.com
  2. Subject: Facebook Data Deletion Request
  3. Include the email linked to your Facebook account and your HOVER account email (if different).

We will process requests within 7 business days, subject to applicable laws (e.g. order/invoice retention).
